# Phase 4 — Realistic Data and Dynamic Booking Simulation

Phases 1-3 developed and validated the MILP models on small synthetic instances
where all boats are known in advance. Phase 4 answers the three questions that
remained open for the thesis:

1. **Does the approach hold on realistic, large-scale marina data?**
2. **What happens when requests arrive over time instead of all at once?**
3. **Which objective weights are best, and in which operating conditions?**

---

## 1. The realistic dataset (`src/realistic/`)

### Marina inventory (`marina.py`)

A synthetic but realistically parameterised marina of **460 wet berths** in 8
size categories (A: up to 8 m ... H: up to 30 m), modelled on the published
characteristics of large Greek marinas (Alimos, Zea, Gouvia, Rhodes class):

| Category | Max boat | Berths | Depth | Power | Base rate (EUR/m/day) | Contract share |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | 8 m  | 90  | 2.2 m | 3.7 kW  | 1.9 | 68% |
| B | 10 m | 100 | 2.6 m | 3.7 kW  | 2.4 | 65% |
| C | 12 m | 95  | 3.0 m | 7.4 kW  | 3.0 | 58% |
| D | 15 m | 80  | 3.4 m | 7.4 kW  | 3.9 | 52% |
| E | 18 m | 45  | 3.8 m | 14.5 kW | 5.0 | 42% |
| F | 21 m | 25  | 4.2 m | 14.5 kW | 6.2 | 38% |
| G | 25 m | 15  | 4.8 m | 45 kW   | 7.5 | 28% |
| H | 30 m | 10  | 5.5 m | 45 kW   | 9.5 | 20% |

A 12 m boat in a C berth pays ~36 EUR/day in shoulder season and ~49 EUR/day
in August (x1.35 seasonal multiplier), matching real Greek tariff sheets.
**258 berths (55%) are blocked by annual contracts**; the allocation problem
concerns the **202-berth transient pool**, as in real marina operations.

### Booking stream (`demand.py`)

Season 1 April - 31 October 2025 (214 days). Requests are generated by an
inhomogeneous Poisson process and each request carries a **booking day**
separate from its **arrival day**:

- monthly demand factors 0.25 (Apr) ... 1.0 (Aug) ... 0.30 (Oct);
- Friday/Saturday arrival peak (charter changeover);
- boat mix: 15% day cruisers (6-9.5 m), 45% sailing cruisers (9.5-17 m),
  30% motor yachts (10-22 m), 10% superyachts (20-29 m), with realistic
  beam/draft/shore-power per segment;
- length of stay: 55% transit (1-3 nights), 30% weekly charter (4-7),
  15% longer, mean ~4.9 nights;
- booking lead time: 35% same-day to 2 days (VHF walk-ins), 40% up to two
  weeks, 25% further ahead; superyachts book earlier.

Scenarios scale the intensity: **low x0.7, base x1.0, high x1.3**. In the base
scenario peak-season demand reaches ~116% of transient capacity, so genuine
rejections occur; the low season is slack; the high season is heavily
oversubscribed.

Datasets are deterministic given (scenario, seed) and exported to
`data/realistic/` as CSV by `experiments/phase4/make_dataset.py`.

## 2. Dynamic simulation (`src/simulation/`)

`engine.simulate_season` replays the booking timeline day by day: requests are
revealed on their booking day, the policy decides (accept + berth, or reject),
accepted bookings are committed to a berth-by-day calendar and never moved.

### Policies (`policies.py`)

| Policy | Decision timing | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| `online[first_fit]` | at booking instant | first free feasible berth in dock-walk order |
| `online[best_fit]` | at booking instant | tightest free feasible berth |
| `online[revenue_max]` | at booking instant | highest-rate free feasible berth (myopic) |
| `batch[...]` | end of batching window | joint assignment MILP over the window's requests |

The batch MILP maximises the sum of **net pair values** over feasible
(berth, request) pairs:

```
net(i, j) = revenue(i, j)
          - space * rate_i * (length_i - length_j) * nights_j      tight packing
          - frag  * rate_i * length_i * dead_gap_days(i, j)        fragmentation
          - opp   * rate_i * length_i * sum_t pressure(t)          opportunity cost
```

- `revenue(i, j)` = rate_i x boat length x seasonal price sum of the stay;
- *dead gap days*: enclosed free runs shorter than 3 days that the booking
  would strand against existing bookings on the berth calendar;
- `pressure(t)` in [0, 1] is the known seasonality; with weight `opp = L` the
  policy accepts a boat in peak season only if it fills more than fraction L
  of the berth's revenue potential — a yield-management protection level.
  A request whose every candidate has non-positive net value is **rejected
  strategically**.

Constraints: one berth per request; on each berth, overlapping stays exclude
each other (one interval-clique constraint per distinct stay start). Solved
with **HiGHS** via `scipy.optimize.milp` on candidate pairs only (the 30 best
per request, half tightest / half highest-rate), so a nightly batch at
460-berth scale solves in milliseconds. Requests arriving before the next
batch epoch are decided the same evening (urgent mini-batch).

### Clairvoyant benchmark

The exact perfect-information MILP over a full season (~700k pairs) is not
solvable to optimality in reasonable time, and even its LP relaxation does not
finish with the interior-point solver. The comparison therefore brackets the
perfect-information optimum with two fast, sound bounds:

- **upper bound** (`category_upper_bound`): berths pooled per size category at
  the category's maximum rate and most permissive dimensions; an LP assigns
  request fractions to pools under per-day capacities. Every feasible
  berth-level plan maps into this LP with no smaller objective, so its optimum
  is a valid upper bound on any policy (inflated ~4% by the within-category
  rate spread). Solves in under a second.
- **feasible clairvoyant plan** (`clairvoyant_greedy`): perfect-information
  greedy processing requests by arrival day with best-fit placement; its
  revenue lower-bounds the clairvoyant optimum.

(all from the project root with `python -X utf8 ...`).

Results and interpretation: see `report/FINAL_REPORT.md`.

### Modelling simplifications

- stern-to mooring only, one boat per berth (no alongside/spanning in phase 4);
- no cancellations or no-shows;
- accepted bookings are never relocated (stable assignment, matching phases
  1-3 findings that stability costs little revenue);
- contract berths are fully blocked all season.
